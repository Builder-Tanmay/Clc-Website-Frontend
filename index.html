Care that combines clinical skill with patience

We started CLC because we believe a developmental delay is not a permanent barrier. Occupational therapists, speech-language pathologists and remedial educators work together under one roof, so your child gets joined-up support instead of separate appointments.

Every plan starts with a careful evaluation, uses play your child enjoys, and includes simple guidance you can follow at home.

  • One-on-one plans built around your child's own pace.
  • Sensory gyms with suspended swings, bolster barrels and fine-motor stations.
  • Parent debriefs after every session, so you know what we worked on.

Your first visit, step by step

Starting therapy can feel like a big step. This is what to expect, so nothing comes as a surprise.

Each session is 40 minutes and ends with a short debrief, so you always know what we worked on and what to practise at home.

  1. Get in touch

    Fill in the short form or message us on WhatsApp. Our coordinator calls you back to choose a centre and a time that suits your family.

  2. Meet the therapist

    Your child is evaluated through play and everyday tasks. We also listen to your concerns and what you have noticed at home or school.

  3. Get a plan in plain language

    We explain what we found, which therapies will help and how often your child should come, without jargon.

  4. Begin sessions and practise at home

    One-on-one sessions begin, with a debrief each time and simple activities to do at home. We review progress with you regularly and adjust the plan.
